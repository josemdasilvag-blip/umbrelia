Umbrelia · live demo

Talk to an AI receptionist that books real appointments

This is the assistant of a demo clinic in Madrid (beauty and dental). It answers questions with the clinic's own prices and opening hours, and books, moves or cancels appointments in its calendar.

Message on WhatsApp

The voice call runs in your browser and needs your microphone. Calls last up to 5 minutes. She speaks English and Spanish: just talk to her in the language you prefer. The same goes for WhatsApp.

Things to ask her on the call, in English or Spanish

  • How much is a teeth whitening?«¿Cuánto cuesta un blanqueamiento dental?»
  • Any slot on Thursday afternoon for a manicure?«¿Tenéis hueco el jueves por la tarde para una manicura?»
  • Actually, move it to Friday morning.«Mejor cámbiamela al viernes por la mañana.»
  • Are you open on Saturday?«¿Abrís el sábado?»
